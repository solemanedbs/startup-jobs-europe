    Three real examples, from modest to substantial
    Shift planner, Excel

    Shifts were planned by hand every week and people found out late. I rebuilt the planning in a shared sheet with automatic totals. The team now sees next week by Thursday, and the manager saves about two hours a week.

    No link, no code, no spectacular number — and it still shows initiative and follow-through.
    Cold email sequence, 400 founders

    We had no pipeline on the UK market. I built the target list from scratch and wrote a four-touch sequence with per-segment personalisation. 18% replied and we booked 12 meetings.

    A link to the sequence or the sheet makes it verifiable.
    This job board

    Finding roles at funded startups meant opening hundreds of career pages. I wrote a script that reads 1,000+ VC portfolios and their applicant tracking systems every morning, and published the result as a free site.

    A public link is the strongest possible proof.
