How Traction works

Every role here is read from the hiring company's own careers page, every morning. Nothing is taken from another job board, and no company pays to appear.

Where the roles come from

Rebuilt every morning
The portfolios

Every morning we re-read the list of companies backed by 1,372 venture capital funds across Europe and North America.

Their own careers pages

For each of the 4,651 companies, we query the applicant tracking system the company itself uses — 14 different systems.

The roles

That gives 51,974 open roles across 84 countries, each with the posting date the company publishes. 37,040 of them were posted in the last 30 days, which is what the job board shows by default.

What we never do

Nothing is taken from another job board. No company pays to appear. No recruiter posts here. A role that disappears from a careers page disappears from here.

My space

An optional, private workspace: your profile, your projects, the CVs you tailor and the applications you track. It needs no account because nothing is stored on this site.

Fill your profile from your CV

The longest step, done once — and it is the one an AI can do for you.

  1. Drop your CV on the Profile screen. It is read on your machine; the file never leaves your browser.
  2. Click Copy the extraction request.
  3. Paste it into whichever AI you use — Claude, ChatGPT, Mistral — together with your CV.
  4. Copy the answer it gives back.
  5. Paste it into the box on the Profile screen and click Fill my profile.
  6. Correct whatever the AI got wrong. Everything stays editable.

Tailor a CV to one job ad

Your profile holds more than fits on a page, so the AI can choose what matters for this ad instead of rewording what you already wrote.

  1. Open Tailor a CV and paste the job ad.
  2. Click Copy the text for the AI.
  3. Paste it into your AI. It writes the CV from the facts in your profile.
  4. Nothing is sent from this page — your facts only move when you copy them yourself.

Share your profile as a link

One link, no account, no page hosted on your behalf.

  1. On the Profile screen, click Get my link.
  2. The link carries your profile inside it — identity, experience, projects, education, skills. Not your applications.
  3. Change your profile and the old link keeps the old version, so generate a new one.

Track your applications

A table, a funnel, and a reminder when something has been waiting too long.

  1. Apply on a company's own site. When you come back, Traction asks whether you applied, and logs it if you say yes.
  2. The Overview screen shows where each application stands, and which ones have been waiting more than ten days with no reply.
  3. Export the whole thing as CSV whenever you want.
Why the copy-paste round trip?
Calling an AI from this page would remove those steps — and would mean sending your CV to a server. Everything in My space lives in your browser alone, so your facts only move when you move them. That is the trade, and it is deliberate.
