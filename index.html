    Every role here is read directly from a company's own career page — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Teamtailor, Personio, Workable, Recruitee and a dozen other applicant tracking systems — and re-read every morning. Nothing is scraped from other job boards, and no role is posted by a recruiter.

    The companies come from the portfolios of more than 1,200 venture capital funds in Europe and the United States. That is the filter: funded companies with money in the bank and real hiring plans. Their offices are not only in Europe — a startup backed from Paris or Berlin may well be hiring in New York, Bangalore or Dubai, so you will find those roles here too.
