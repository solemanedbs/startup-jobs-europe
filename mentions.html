Data

This site collects no personal data, sets no cookie and asks for no sign-up. Traffic is measured anonymously and in aggregate by GoatCounter (no cookie, no identifier, IP addresses not retained). Anything you enter in “My space” — your profile, projects and applications — is stored in your own browser (localStorage) and is never transmitted to this site or to anyone else.

Content

Job listings are reproduced from companies' own public career pages, through the interfaces their recruitment systems make available (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Teamtailor, Personio, Workable, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, Pinpoint, Breezy, Workday, Join, BambooHR, Rippling). Every listing links back to its source, and applications are made exclusively on the company's own site. Job titles, locations and descriptions remain the property of the companies concerned. A company that would rather not appear here can write to the address above; removal is done within 48 hours.
