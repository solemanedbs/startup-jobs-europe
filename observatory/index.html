Startup hiring in numbers — Traction Observatory

Counts on venture-backed hiring that nobody else publishes, read every morning from the companies' own careers pages. Nothing modelled, nothing estimated, and no trends claimed we cannot support.

In short

How many startups are hiring right now?

As of 2026-11-27, 4,379 venture-backed companies had 48,652 roles open, counted directly from their own careers pages across 83 countries.

Where are most startup jobs?

San Francisco and New York hold 14% of all open roles. United States alone accounts for 51%.

Are startup jobs remote?

Mostly not. 15.9% of roles at venture-backed companies are advertised as remote as of 2026-11-27. Sales roles are the most remote-friendly, engineering among the least.

Do startup job posts show salary?

20.7% do. Four in five do not. The share ranges from 29% in United States to 1.5% in India.

Which applicant tracking system do startups use?

Ashby, used by 31.4% of these companies, ahead of Greenhouse at 23.7%.

Do a few companies do most of the hiring?

Yes. The top 10% of companies hold 47% of all open roles, while 745 companies — 17% — are hiring for exactly one person.

Traction reads the careers page of every venture-backed company it can find, every morning. That produces counts nobody else publishes. Here is what today's reading says about 48,652 open roles at 4,379 funded companies.

The daily series

Traction has recorded a reading every morning since 2026-09-22 — 55 of them. No other public source on startup hiring publishes daily: Ashby reports once a year on 1,200 startups, Carta on about 3,000, and the rest are point-in-time samples.

The line below is not a hiring index, and reading it as one would be wrong. Between September and November the total went from 16,854 to 48,652 roles — that is mostly Traction adding companies, not the market tripling. Coverage and market have to be separated, so they are.

The panel holds the same 4,443 companies every day, whether they are hiring or not. Today 4,173 of them have something open.

Hiring is concentrated

Of 4,379 companies with something open, 47% of all roles sit with the top 10% (the top 1% hire 10% · the top 5% hire 30% · the top 10% hire 47% · the top 25% hire 72%). The median company has 5 roles open, and 745 of them — 17% — are hiring for exactly one person. A startup job market is mostly a long tail of companies making a single hire.

How this is counted

Every number on this page is a count of job posts read directly from each company's own careers page, every morning. Nothing is modelled, estimated, weighted or projected. A role that comes down disappears the next day.

There are no trends here, on purpose. Traction has only been observing since 25 September 2026, and the publication dates on older posts suffer from survivorship bias — a role posted in April and still open is a role nobody took. Claiming a hiring trend from that would be wrong, so these are dated snapshots instead.

The companies come from the published portfolios of venture funds, and only those whose system exposes a public job feed can be read. This describes venture-backed hiring, not the labour market.
