What this is not

This measures whether a pay range appears in the post. It says nothing about whether the range is honest, how wide it is, or what the job actually pays. A range of "$80k – $400k" counts here exactly like a tight one.

Only countries with at least 400 roles in this sample are shown; below that a handful of large employers moves the figure too much to mean anything.

Counted from job posts read directly from each company's own careers page on 2026-11-15. Nothing modelled, nothing estimated.
